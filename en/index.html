About the author

Ellian Lessel

Ellian Lessel is a Montevideo-based author whose work spans three territories that rarely share a shelf: epic science fiction, the strategic analysis of sport, and the scientific essay on the future of civilizations. A single question ties them together: how complex systems think, decide, and endure, whether a people abandoning its world, a team on the pitch, or a species looking up at the stars.

His outlook was shaped by the study of strategy. He holds a bachelor’s degree in Military Sciences and is a professor of the History of Armed Conflicts. He holds an MBA and a master’s degree in National Strategy, along with postgraduate specializations in Strategic Intelligence and in Security and Defense.

He teaches in the Master’s in National Strategy and in the postgraduate specialization programs of Uruguay’s Center for Advanced National Studies (CALEN) and the Military Institute of Higher Studies (IMES). There he teaches how to read conflict, decision-making, and the long term, the same keys that sustain his writing.

That background runs through each of his books. In the Ad Primordia saga, which opens with Despertar, the comparative mythology of Sumer, Babylon, Assyria, Persia, and the Judeo-Christian tradition meets hard science fiction and first contact. In El tablero de césped he translates the vocabulary of chess (the pin, the fork, zugzwang) into an operating system for analyzing soccer in real time. In La eficiencia espaciotemporal he proposes a Master Equation for classifying civilizations, in dialogue with Kardashev, Sagan, Drake, and Fermi.

He writes with a conviction shared by all three books: an idea is worth only as much as it withstands scrutiny. That is why he offers not closed truths but frameworks for thinking, and invites readers to debate them, recalculate them, and challenge them.
